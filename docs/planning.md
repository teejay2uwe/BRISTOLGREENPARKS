# Project Proposal

## Business Case "ParkFinder Bristol"

### Problem statement
An app that aims to help people identify parks and green spaces around Bristol, especially for those who struggle to locate nearby options. It also provides detailed information about specific needs such as dog-friendly areas, disability accessibility, sports and play facilities, and playgrounds.

### Business benefits
* Helps people find parks and green spaces easily 
* Encourages people to get outside, be active and exercise 
### Options Considered
* Google searches/maps - good for navigation but can lack detailed information about parks 
* Bristol City Council website – contains information about parks difficult to use and search for 
### Expected Risks
* Would have to compete with other map apps/product 

* Relies on the Bristol Open data set so will need to be maintained and updated
